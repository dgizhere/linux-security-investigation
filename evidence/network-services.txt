# NETWORK SERVICE INVESTIGATION

Command:
sudo ss -ltnp

Purpose:
Identify listening TCP sockets, numeric addresses and ports,
and the processes associated with them.

Relevant finding:

127.0.0.1:631
Process: cupsd
PID: 1569

Interpretation:

Port 631 was associated with the CUPS printing service.

The service was bound to the loopback address 127.0.0.1,
meaning the listener was bound to the local machine rather
than directly exposed through all network interfaces.

Security observation:

A service bound only to the loopback interface has a smaller
network exposure than a service listening on all IPv4
interfaces.

Note:
This evidence describes the observed listener and does not
by itself establish that the service is secure or vulnerable.
